Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Jun Automation ("the App") is a private internal automation tool operated solely by its owner for publishing educational Korean-learning short-form videos to the owner's own social media accounts.

1. Data Accessed

The App uses OAuth 2.0 credentials authorized strictly by the App owner to upload video files and metadata (titles, descriptions, privacy status, and AI-content disclosures) via official platform APIs, including Google Drive API, YouTube Data API v3, Meta Graph API, and TikTok Content Posting API.

2. How Data Is Used

Access tokens and uploaded media metadata are used solely to publish and track the owner's own educational videos. The App does not collect, sell, rent, or share any personal data from third-party users.

3. Data Storage and Security

OAuth credentials are stored exclusively as encrypted secrets in the owner's private repository and cloud environment. No external user accounts or third-party databases are maintained.

4. Revoking Access

The owner may revoke the App's API permissions at any time from Google Account Security settings, Meta Business settings, or TikTok account settings.

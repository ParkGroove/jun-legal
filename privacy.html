Privacy Policy — Jun Automation

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Jun Automation ("the App") is a private internal automation tool operated solely by its owner for uploading original educational Korean-learning short-form videos to the owner's own creator channels.

1. YouTube API Services & Google Privacy Policy

This App uses YouTube API Services (specifically YouTube Data API v3 videos.insert with scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload) strictly to upload the owner's own videos. By using YouTube API Services, use is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms).

2. Data Accessed and Used

The App uses OAuth 2.0 credentials authorized exclusively by the owner to upload original video files and metadata (title, description, category, privacy status, and synthetic media disclosure). The App does not collect, access, cache, sell, or share any third-party user data or third-party YouTube channel data.

3. Data Retention, Deletion Policy & Revoking Access

The App only records the owner's own uploaded video_id in a private ledger for deduplication. No third-party YouTube data is stored. All stored metadata can be deleted immediately by the owner at any time. In addition, the owner can revoke the App's access to Google/YouTube data at any time via the Google Security Settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
